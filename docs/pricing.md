## Apify Platform & Actor Pricing
The CLI interacts with the official Apify cloud scraping service ([apify.com](https://apify.com)) to execute the Product Hunt Scraper actor (`cloud9_ai/producthunt-scraper`). Apify platform pricing is managed directly by Apify:
- **Free Plan**: Apify includes free monthly prepaid usage credits every month, which is typically sufficient for hundreds of searches and scrapes.
- **Paid Plans**: For heavy automated scraping or commercial workflows requiring higher concurrency, Apify provides tiered subscriptions (Starter, Scale, Business).
- **Actor Usage**: The scraper actor consumes minimal compute units per run based on execution duration (typically 30–60 seconds per batch).

For official Apify subscription details and credit pricing, visit [https://apify.com/pricing](https://apify.com/pricing).
